Independent companion activity · Brainy Bucks: Math & Reading

Parent card: find the clue, then explain it

Independent parent-guided activity. Read: “The path was soggy after the rain, and water squeezed out when Lina stepped on it.” Ask what soggy probably means: wet, narrow, or noisy. Before choosing, ask the child to point to one useful detail. Answer: wet; the rain and squeezed-out water support it. Now replace soggy with muddy and discuss whether both words mean exactly the same thing. They do not: mud includes soft, wet earth. Finish by inventing one new sentence that makes soggy understandable without defining it. This is an original paper activity, not a reproduced Brainy Bucks question. Adults can explore the app's separate reading practice afterward.

Independent companion activity · Family Ready: Emergency Plan

A household handoff map without private details

Independent planning activity: draw a table with three columns: information category, who may need it, and where the current copy is kept. Use category labels only, such as contacts, pet routine and household checklist. Do not write real names, numbers, addresses or medical details on this practice sheet. For a fictional pet routine, the recipient might be 'agreed helper' and the location 'private household record.' Mark any row whose recipient or storage location is still undecided. The result is a map of questions to resolve, not an emergency plan or a safety assessment. Family Ready offers separate tools for organizing the actual records privately.

Independent companion activity · RoomMend: Safer Home Plans

Rewrite a vague room note so it can be found again

Independent record-writing activity: start with the fictional note 'look at that mark.' Add four fields: room, exact visible location, observation date and what was seen. Example: 'Living room; lower left corner of the interior wall beside the shelf; November 6; small grey mark visible in daylight.' Keep the description observational; do not invent a cause or a repair method. Add one next step, such as documenting it for an appropriate assessment. The finished note should identify the same spot a week later without relying on memory. RoomMend provides native room records, photos and follow-up states for keeping that information together.
