How long did
story club last?

Draw hops from each start to its finish. Label each hop in minutes, then add the labels. All visits happen on one day. Every time without an AM/PM label is in the same morning or afternoon.

Try one together

Story club runs from 10:48 to 11:17. Count to the next hour, then on to the finish.

12 + 17 = 29 minutes. Different hops are fine if they reach the finish and count every minute once.

Facilitator key

For family library programs or a short paired session. No timer, name, grade or score is needed. This is practice, not a learning assessment.

Open the three explained answers
  1. Comic swap: 32 minutes. From 9:36 to 10:00 is 24 minutes; from 10:00 to 10:08 is 8 more. 24 + 8 = 32.
  2. Shadow theatre: 35 minutes. From 11:46 AM to noon is 14 minutes; from noon to 12:21 PM is 21 more. 14 + 21 = 35.
  3. Double feature: 47 minutes of activity. The talk lasts 25 minutes (10 to 2:00, then 15). Drawing lasts 22 minutes. 25 + 22 = 47. The break is 10 minutes. The full visit lasts 57 minutes including the break.

Run an eight-minute partner session

Spend one minute reading the example aloud. Give pairs four minutes to draw their hops, then spend three minutes comparing routes. Ask each pair what the numbers on one hop mean. These are suggested timings, not a test.

If a hop gets stuck

If someone subtracts 36 from 8, ask which time happens first. Put the times on the line and count forward. A pair can split a hop at any convenient minute and check that the labels still add to the same total. For the final puzzle, mark the break separately before adding.
