    Questions about keeping a household plan

    Where do I find instructions during a storm?

    Check your local emergency management authority and official weather alerts. For immediate danger, contact your local emergency number. This worksheet is for planning ahead.

    What if someone uses powered medical equipment?

    Before an outage, ask the healthcare professional or equipment provider to help prepare a device-specific plan. Keep the instructions and contact numbers where the household can find them.

    Can the Emergency Card work offline?

    Family Ready’s free Emergency Card opens in Emergency Mode without internet once the phone is unlocked. The free dated Emergency Snapshot PDF gives you a paper option.

    Does Family Ready call for help automatically?

    No. Family Ready organizes the information you enter. You can tap a contact to start a call yourself.

    Which features require Family Ready Plus?

    The Emergency Card, household plan, kit checklist and Emergency Snapshot PDF are free. Trusted-person invitations, household sync, guided reviews and additional printable packs are among the optional Plus features.
