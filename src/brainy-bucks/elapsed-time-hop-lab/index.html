    TRY THIS ONE TOGETHER

    9:40 to 10:25
    is a pair of hops.

    Reach the next hour.

    From 9:40 to 10:00 is 20 minutes. The hour ends at 60 minutes, not 100.

    Reach the finish.

    From 10:00 to 10:25 is another 25 minutes.

    Add the parts.

    20 + 25 = 45 minutes altogether. You have counted the full interval without subtracting the clock labels as decimals.

    When the gap is longer

    Keep hopping through each full hour, then add the last part. From 10:50 AM to 12:15 PM, the hops are 10 minutes to 11:00, 60 minutes to noon and 15 minutes to the finish: 85 minutes.

    The short hand moves gradually between hour numbers. For example, at 9:30 it sits halfway between 9 and 10. The long hand points to the minutes.
